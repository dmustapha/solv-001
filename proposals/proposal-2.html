How it works

Three-layer financial design

Every USDC that enters or leaves solv-001 follows a deliberate protocol. Income, expenses, and capital management are handled by separate cryptographic mechanisms.

↓
EIP-3009 Income

Clients sign a TransferWithAuthorization off-chain. Circle's Gateway settles the USDC transfer — no approval transaction, no MetaMask popups mid-flow.

1 Client signs EIP-3009 typed data in their wallet
2 Signature sent to Circle Gateway /v1/payments/settle
3 USDC credited to agent wallet on Arc testnet
→
Direct Arc RPC

Agent queries Arc testnet directly for blockchain data. Expenses are tracked internally and recorded to the treasury ledger for full transparency.

1 Agent calls Arc RPC for tx counts, logs, bytecode
2 Claude processes raw data into structured insights
3 Cost recorded to treasury ledger ($0.005–$0.015)
◈
USYC Yield Engine

Idle USDC above the $10 operating reserve is automatically deposited into Hashnote USYC, earning ~4.85% APY. Redeemed automatically when reserves dip low.

1 Post-task: check if balance > reserve × 1.5
2 Approve + deposit via Teller contract on Arc
3 USYC minted, accruing ~4.85% APY passively
Pricing

Pay per task, in USDC

All payments are EIP-3009 signed authorizations settled by Circle Gateway on Arc testnet. No subscriptions, no gas for the client.
